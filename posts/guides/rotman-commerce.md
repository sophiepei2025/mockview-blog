## U of T Rotman Commerce Requirements & Admissions Process

The Bachelor of Commerce (BCom) is a **four-year program** offered jointly by U of T's **Rotman School of Management** and the **Faculty of Arts & Science**.

Students gain the tools, knowledge, and experience needed for a career in business, accounting, investment banking, and beyond. From day one, students build a foundation in economics that informs their understanding of business theory, with an emphasis on developing as leaders and engaged citizens.

### How to Apply

All prospective students apply to the University of Toronto's **Faculty of Arts & Science** at the **St. George (downtown Toronto) campus** through the [Ontario Universities' Application Centre (OUAC)](https://www.ouac.on.ca/).

There are a few different OUAC applications depending on your situation:

- **101 Application**: For Ontario high school students
- **105 Application**: For Canadian or international students not currently enrolled at an Ontario high school, applying to more than one university in Ontario
- **International Application**: For applicants living outside Canada who haven't studied in Canada and aren't applying to any other Ontario university

All applicants apply to U of T, select the Faculty of Arts & Science at the St. George campus, and then choose Rotman Commerce from the program list.

  <strong>Worth knowing:</strong> Submitting your OUAC 101 application early does not improve your admission chances — the only difference is you may receive a decision sooner. About 1–2 weeks after submitting your OUAC application, you'll receive an email from U of T confirming receipt and explaining how to access **JOIN U of T**. In addition to your OUAC application, all Rotman Commerce applicants must complete a **Supplemental Application** through JOIN U of T, submitted via a third-party video interview platform.

### Rotman Commerce Admission Average

Rotman uses your **top six Grade 12 U/M courses** to calculate your admission average.

Rotman publishes a minimum average cutoff for consideration, but the competitive range tends to run meaningfully higher than that minimum — the gap between "meets the cutoff" and "competitive" is real. **Because these numbers shift from year to year, the most reliable source is Rotman's own admissions page** rather than any average quoted in a guide like this one.

This is exactly where the **Supplemental Application** matters most: even an applicant without a top-tier average can move into the competitive zone with a strong interview, while a high average paired with a weak Supp App can lose ground to someone who prepared better for that part.

### Rotman Commerce Acceptance Rate

Rotman Commerce is a **competitive, high-demand program** — Arts & Science overall receives a large volume of applications relative to available seats, and Rotman Commerce is one of its more sought-after streams.

The precise acceptance rate moves year to year based on applicant volume and how many admitted students accept their offer, so rather than quote a number here, your best move is to check Rotman's own admissions data or ask directly during an info session.

### Rotman Commerce Acceptance Dates

Rotman Commerce evaluates applicants in the spring and sends most offers out over a period of a few weeks — students who applied for early consideration typically hear sooner. A further, smaller round of offers usually follows a few weeks later, once Rotman has a clearer picture of how many admitted students accepted their spot. If you don't hear in the first round, it doesn't mean you've been declined — waitlist movement is normal for this program.

## About the Rotman Commerce Program

### Guaranteed Admission (GA)

Some Rotman Commerce applicants are admitted through a Guaranteed Admission stream tied to specific partner high schools and program criteria. If you're unsure whether this applies to you, check with your school's guidance counselor or Rotman's admissions office directly — GA eligibility criteria can change, so don't rely on secondhand information.

### Specialists

Rotman Commerce students choose from several areas of specialization as they progress through the program, spanning areas like finance, accounting, management, and marketing. Course offerings and specialist options are updated periodically, so the current list on Rotman's own site is the accurate reference.

### Internships & Study Abroad

Rotman Commerce students have access to internship opportunities and international exchange programs during their degree. Program partners, application windows, and eligibility details change fairly often, so check Rotman's current internship and exchange pages when you're ready to plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the classes like?**
Expect a mix of large first- and second-year lecture courses building your foundation in economics and business theory, narrowing into smaller, specialist-focused classes by third and fourth year.

**How do I choose a specialist?**
Most students don't lock in a specialist right away — the first year or two is designed to expose you to different areas (finance, accounting, marketing, management) before you have to commit.

**Is Rotman hard?**
It's a demanding program academically, and the culture is competitive — but "hard" plays out differently for different students depending on study habits and how well the specialist you choose fits your strengths.
