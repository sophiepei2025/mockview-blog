## UBC Sauder — Requirements & Admissions Process

The Sauder BCom is a four-year program with a strong focus on experiential learning and personal/professional development — leadership, critical thinking, and communication built in alongside the business fundamentals.

The application has three components:

1. **Online application** through EducationPlannerBC
2. **Personal Profile**
3. **Video interview**

### Admission Requirements & Average

UBC has general admission requirements that apply regardless of program (minimum English grade, recommended course load), plus Sauder-specific requirements on top. Sauder considers your Grade 11 and 12 courses, and while there's a general sense of what a competitive average looks like, it isn't a hard cutoff — Sauder receives far more applications at or above that range than it has seats, which is exactly why the Personal Profile and interview carry real weight. A strong Profile and interview can push an application into the competitive zone even with a slightly lower average — and the reverse is also true.

### Acceptance Rate & Timeline

Sauder BCom is a competitive program relative to the number of available seats, though the exact acceptance rate shifts from year to year based on applicant volume and how many admitted students accept their offer — check Sauder's own admissions data for the current numbers rather than a fixed figure. Decisions typically go out on a rolling basis over a period of a few months; if you don't hear back right away, that's normal, since UBC continues reviewing and sending offers (including after some admitted students decline) later into the cycle.

## UBC Sauder Application: Personal Profile & Video Interview

The application (submitted through EducationPlannerBC) has two core components:

- **Personal Profile** — two Commerce-specific short-essay questions plus a set of questions asked of all UBC applicants, covering your experiences, interests, and skills, along with an activities list and two references.
- **Timed video responses** — a small set of randomly generated interview questions; you typically respond to more than you ultimately submit, giving you some ability to pick your strongest response.

<div class="institution-callout">
  Sauder's evaluation looks well beyond grades — communication, self-awareness, and how clearly you can convey your own goals and experiences all factor in. Since a large share of applicants already meet the academic bar, the Personal Profile and interview are frequently what separates an accepted application from a declined one.
</div>

## About the UBC Sauder BCom Program

### Specializations & Courses

Sauder BCom students choose from a range of specializations as they progress through the program, spanning areas like finance, marketing, accounting, and entrepreneurship — course offerings and specialization options are best checked on Sauder's current program pages, since they're periodically updated.

### Co-op

Second- and third-year BCom students can pursue a co-op, finding their own placement for a work term (paid, full-time) that counts toward their degree on a pass/fail basis. It's a genuine opportunity to build professional experience and a network before graduating, alongside earning income during the term.

### Scholarships & Financial Aid

UBC and Sauder both offer a range of entrance scholarships and awards — some general university-wide awards, others Sauder/BCom-specific, plus dedicated funding for Indigenous and international students. Amounts, eligibility, and deadlines change from year to year, so Sauder's own financial aid pages are the reliable source rather than a number quoted here.

### Graduate & Continuing Studies Options

Sauder offers a range of paths after the BCom — several Master's programs (MBA, Master of Management, Master of Business Analytics, and others), a PhD in Business Administration, dual-degree options combining an undergraduate degree with a Master of Management, and professional development programs and diplomas (including real estate credentialing and Ch'nook Indigenous business education programs). Worth exploring directly on Sauder's site if continuing beyond the BCom is something you're considering.
